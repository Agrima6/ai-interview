            Executive                 Summary                                                                                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
          Your   organization      is looking   to strengthen     its cybersecurity      posture    and  support    secure,    resilient  business     operations.     Workmate      proposes     a    
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
          structured    cyber    security   assessment       covering    internal   and  external    infrastructure,    web   applications,     APIs,   wireless    networks,    firewalls,   and      
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
          source    code   —   identifying    vulnerabilities,    validating   exposure,     and   guiding   remediation      before   they   can   be  exploited.                                     
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
          One-time     assessment       across    all in-scope    areas,   followed    by  a  revalidation    assessment       to confirm    remediation.                                              
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                       
          Testing    aligned    with  OWASP,       NIST,   and   industry-standard       methodologies,       with   findings   mapped      to CVSS/CVE.
